# AILMR — AI Labor Market Research (Interactive Presentation)

This repository contains the **interactive presentation** for my AI Labor Market Research (AILMR) project. 

AILMR is a fully local ecosystem for job seekers in Germany. It goes beyond simple job parsing and covers the entire application journey: from market research and ATS-ready CV checks to AI-generated cover letters and STAR-method interview training.

### 🚀 What is inside?
This is not just a static PDF. It is a fully interactive web presentation built with HTML/JS and Plotly. It includes:
- **Market Analytics:** Interactive charts analyzing the German AI job market.
- **Skill Mapping:** A breakdown of 19 AI tools and how they correlate with job titles.
- **Ecosystem Overview:** A look at the local AI features (Ollama for text, Whisper for voice).
- **Multilingual Support:** Switch between EN, DE, UA, and RU directly in the UI.
